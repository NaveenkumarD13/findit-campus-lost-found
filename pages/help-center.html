Frequently Asked Questions

Tap a question to expand the answer

How do I create a FindIt account?

Click "Sign Up" on the homepage or login page. Fill in your full name, role (Student, Faculty, or Admin), department, campus email (@gmail.com), mobile number, and a secure password. After submitting, you'll receive a 6-digit OTP on your email — enter it in the verification modal to complete registration. Your account is created instantly and you're logged in automatically.

Why didn't I receive my OTP code?

OTP codes are displayed directly on-screen for demo purposes (look for the highlighted code in the hint box). In a production environment, the code would be sent to your registered email. If you don't see the hint, make sure you entered a valid @gmail.com address. You can also click "Resend OTP" after the 60-second timer expires to generate a new code.

How do I report a lost item?

Navigate to "Report Lost Item" from the homepage or the floating action button. Fill in the item name, category, last-seen location, date lost, your contact number, and a description with identifying details. You can also drag-and-drop or click to upload a photo. Click "Publish Lost Item Report" and your listing goes live instantly across the campus network.

What should I do if I found someone's item?

Go to "Report Found Item" and fill out the form with the item details, where you found it, and your contact information. Upload a photo if possible. Once published, the item appears in the Found Items Gallery on the same page and on the homepage activity feed. The matching engine will automatically compare it against existing lost reports and notify the owner if there's a potential match.

How does the matching engine work?

When you report a found item (or use Smart Search), our engine compares it against all active lost reports by category, item name similarity, and location proximity. Each match receives a confidence score. If the score is above 40%, a "Possible Match Found" modal appears showing both items side-by-side with a confidence percentage. You can then view the owner's contact details to arrange a return.

How do I verify ownership when returning an item?

Always ask the claimer to provide specific identifying details that weren't visible in the public listing — for example, stickers or scratches on a laptop, contents inside a wallet, or the brand of earbuds. Meet in a public campus location during daytime hours. The original report's description field often contains verification clues that only the true owner would know.

Can I log in with OTP instead of a password?

Yes! On the login page, click the "OTP Login" tab. Enter your campus email and click "Send OTP." A 6-digit code will be generated (shown on-screen for the demo). Enter the code within 60 seconds to log in instantly. If the timer expires, click "Resend OTP" to get a new code. OTP login works even if you haven't registered — it creates a guest session.

How do I reset my password?

On the login page, enter your email in the password tab and click "Forgot Password?" — a reset link simulation will appear. In a production environment, this would send a secure reset link to your registered email. If you're an admin, you can use the admin credentials (admin@findit.com / admin123) to access the dashboard and manage reports.

What is Smart Search and how do I use it?

Smart Search is our Google-Images-style object detection page. Type an item name (like "wallet," "laptop," or "keys") or click a quick-tag button. The system displays a grid of matching images from our database. Select an image, then click "Find Matches" to run the matching engine against all lost reports. If a match is found, you'll see a comparison modal with confidence score and owner contact options.

Why don't I see any matches in Smart Search?

Matches depend on existing lost reports in the system. If no one has reported a matching lost item yet, no match will appear. Try reporting a lost item first (from the Report Lost page), then use Smart Search to find it. The matching engine requires at least one lost report with a similar category or name to produce a match.
